# Tailor Master — Customer Guide

## Browsing & buying
1. **Browse** the catalog and open a product.
2. Choose **Ready size** (with a size recommendation) or **Made-to-measure** (your measurements).
3. **Customize** — fabric, collar, cuff, sleeve, embroidery and more. The price and lead time update live; some choices need tailor approval.
4. **Add to cart** and check out.

## Measurements
Save one or more **body profiles** (yours, family). Enter measurements by:
- **Manual** — tape on the body (a guide shows where).
- **Existing garment** — measure a garment that fits you well.
- **Questionnaire** — height, weight, fit preference.

We check your entries: unusual values are flagged, critical measurements ask for a **second entry** to confirm, and you get a **confidence score**. For made-to-measure orders a **tailor reviews and locks** the measurements before cutting begins.

> Body vs garment measurements are different: the finished garment adds ease, your fit preference, fabric behaviour and allowances on top of your body measurements.

## Try your style (Style Preview)
Upload a full-body photo (with your consent) to see the garment's **look** on you.
- This is a **Style Preview only — not a guaranteed fit.** For fit, rely on measurements and tailor review.
- Your image is stored securely and you can **delete the original and the result** anytime.

## Orders
Track each order through production (measurement lock → cutting → stitching → QC → dispatch). Reorder a fit that worked with one tap.

## Privacy
- We ask for **explicit consent** before processing any photo.
- **Delete My Data** and **Delete Uploaded Image** are available in your account.
- Body images and measurements are treated as sensitive and encrypted.

## Languages & accessibility
English and Urdu (right-to-left) are supported, with Arabic-ready layout. The app is mobile-first, keyboard-navigable, and works in light and dark themes.
